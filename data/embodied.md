## Embodied Motion Recovery & Robot Tracking

**From recovery to execution.** This focused collection connects RGB video / egocentric hand reconstruction (e.g., SMPL/SMPL-X/MANO and hand-object trajectories) with robot retargeting, imitation, and motion tracking. It is a bridge to robotics, **not** a general robotics/VLA index.

**Reading guide:** **Vision → Robot** denotes a video-to-robot pipeline; **Recovery** denotes upstream perception only; **Retargeting / Teleoperation** transfers a motion reference; **Tracking** executes the reference via a robot controller. Some systems use XR or MoCap instead of video, and are labeled accordingly. Code links describe what is **actually released**, not necessarily the whole paper pipeline. This manually curated section lives in [`data/embodied.md`](data/embodied.md) and is automatically inserted into `README.md`; it is not included in the auto-harvested paper count.

### Whole-Body Recovery → Humanoid Robots

1. **MoViC: Real-Time Humanoid Whole-Body Control from Monocular Video Streams** — 2026. **Vision → Robot:** live monocular RGB reconstruction, retargeting and asynchronous humanoid motion tracking. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19998) · [Project](https://movicproject.github.io/)
2. **VideoMimic: Visual Imitation Enables Contextual Humanoid Control** — CoRL 2025. **Vision → Robot:** reconstructs human motion and scene geometry from monocular video, then retargets and learns scene-aware humanoid imitation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03729) · [Code](https://github.com/hongsukchoi/VideoMimic)
3. **HumanPlus: Humanoid Shadowing and Imitation from Humans** — CoRL 2024. **Vision → Robot:** uses external body/hand pose estimators for real-time humanoid shadowing and imitation-data collection. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10454) · [Code](https://github.com/MarkFzp/humanplus)
4. **ExMR: Extremity Motion Refinement for Monocular Human Motion Capture** — 2026. **Recovery → Retargeting:** monocular body/hand/foot motion in SMPL-X, with humanoid export through GMR; its repository currently provides demo code, **not** training/evaluation code. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26213) · [Code (demo)](https://github.com/twehrbein/ExMR)
5. **GVHMR: World-Grounded Human Motion Recovery via Gravity-View Coordinates** — SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 / TPAMI 2026. **Recovery:** monocular video to world-grounded human motion, useful upstream of robot retargeting. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06662) · [Code](https://github.com/zju3dv/GVHMR)
6. **OnlineHMR: Video-based Online World-Grounded Human Mesh Recovery** — CVPR 2026. **Recovery:** causal, streaming video-to-SMPL motion; a perception component, **not** a demonstrated robot tracking policy. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17355) · [Code](https://github.com/Tsukasane/Video-OnlineHMR)
7. **GMR: General Motion Retargeting** — ICRA 2026. **Retargeting:** maps human motion (including GVHMR outputs) onto multiple humanoid embodiments, including Unitree G1; it is a bridge, not an HMR method. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02252) · [Code](https://github.com/YanjieZe/GMR)
8. **TWIST: Teleoperated Whole-Body Imitation System** — CoRL 2025. **Tracking:** a whole-body humanoid reference tracker trained with RL/BC from retargeted human MoCap data. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02833) · [Code](https://github.com/YanjieZe/TWIST)
9. **TWIST2: Scalable, Portable, and Holistic Humanoid Data Collection System** — ICRA 2026. **XR Teleoperation → Tracking:** PICO 4 Ultra motion input, retargeting and whole-body tracking on Unitree G1; not camera-based mesh recovery. Teleoperation code is available, while the higher-level policy-learning component is separate. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02832) · [Code](https://github.com/amazon-far/TWIST2)
10. **SONIC: Supersizing Motion Tracking for Natural Humanoid Whole-Body Control** — Science Robotics 2026. **Downstream Tracking:** a generalist humanoid motion tracker that can execute recovered human motion references; **not** a mesh-recovery model. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07820) · [Project](https://nvlabs.github.io/GEAR-SONIC/) · [Models](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/GEAR-SONIC)

### Hand-Object Recovery → Dexterous Robots

1. **Hand2Robot: Object-Centric 4D Hand Reconstruction from Human Demonstrations for Robotics** — 2026. **Vision → Robot:** object-centric 4D hand-object recovery and robot-action supervision through contact refinement and retargeting; **paper link only (code not confirmed).** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15769)
2. **OmniHOI: Dexterous Hand-Object Interaction from Monocular Human Video** — 2026. **Vision → Robot:** monocular hand-object reconstruction, contact-aware retargeting and physics-refined dexterous robot trajectories; **paper link only (code not confirmed).** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10855)
3. **VideoManip: Dexterous Manipulation Policies from RGB Human Videos via 4D Hand-Object Trajectory Reconstruction** — 2026. **Vision → Robot (partial code):** reconstructs 3D hand-object trajectories from RGB video and retargets to robot hands, including LEAP Hand. **Reconstruction/retargeting code is released; grasping and manipulation-policy training are not yet released.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09013) · [Code (reconstruction only)](https://github.com/hychen-naza/VideoManip)
4. **Do as I Do: Dexterous Manipulation Data from Everyday Human Videos** — 2026. **Vision → Robot:** reconstructs hand-object demonstrations from monocular RGB videos, retargets them and supports real-world robot replay. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19333) · [Code](https://github.com/malik-group/do-as-i-do)
5. **UniDex: A Robot Foundation Suite for Universal Dexterous Hand Control from Egocentric Human Videos** — CVPR 2026. **Hand Tracking → Robot Policy:** converts egocentric human-hand data to multiple robot hands; a downstream robot learning suite rather than a new hand mesh estimator. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22264) · [Code](https://github.com/unidex-ai/UniDex)
6. **MINT: A Unified Model for World-Space Camera and Hand Motion Estimation from Scalable Egocentric Pipeline Supervision** — 2026. **Recovery:** recovers camera and two-hand motion in world coordinates from egocentric videos, suitable as input to downstream robot-hand retargeting. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04958) · [Code](https://github.com/wuji-technology/wuji-ego-mint)
7. **HaWoR: World-Space Hand Motion Reconstruction from Egocentric Videos** — CVPR 2025. **Recovery:** reconstructs world-coordinate hand trajectories from egocentric videos as a useful upstream hand-motion reference for robotics. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02973) · [Code](https://github.com/ThunderVVV/HaWoR)
8. **ManipTrans: Efficient Dexterous Bimanual Manipulation Transfer via Residual Learning** — CVPR 2025. **Retargeting → Tracking:** transfers bimanual human hand-object demonstrations to physically feasible robot-hand motions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21860) · [Code](https://github.com/ManipTrans/ManipTrans)
9. **DexMV: Imitation Learning for Dexterous Manipulation from Human Videos** — ECCV 2022. **Recovery → Retargeting → Imitation:** recovers and transfers human hand-object demonstrations for dexterous robot manipulation learning. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.05877) · [Code](https://github.com/yzqin/dexmv-sim)
10. **DexTrack: Towards Generalizable Neural Tracking Control for Dexterous Manipulation from Human References** — ICLR 2025. **Downstream Tracking:** generalizable dexterous robot tracking from human-object references; a controller rather than a hand reconstruction method. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09614) · [Code](https://github.com/Meowuu7/DexTrack)
11. **AdaDexTrack: Dynamic Modulation for Adaptive and Generalizable Dexterous Manipulation Tracking** — CVPR 2026. **Downstream Tracking:** tracks and corrects retargeted, language-generated hand-object references on robot hardware; **not** a hand mesh reconstruction method. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33852) · [Code](https://github.com/Janebek/AdaDexTrack)

### Reusable Retargeting Toolkit

- **dex-retargeting (AnyTeleop)** — **Retargeting:** reusable optimizers translating estimated human-hand motion into robot-hand joint targets; supports video- and dataset-based examples. [Code](https://github.com/dexsuite/dex-retargeting)
